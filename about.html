Behind the lens

Photography and filmmaking portfolio of a student photographer and filmmaker based in San Fernando, Pampanga.

The short version

Film & photography enthusiast, still learning with every project.

I'm a film and photography enthusiast — I enjoy capturing moments and editing them with my own creative style. I have intermediate-level experience in both photography and videography, from shooting to editing. I'm also looking to take on media roles within organizations to keep developing as a photographer and videographer.

01

Documentation First

Most of my work started as event and organization coverage — shooting what's actually happening, not staging it.

02

Full Pipeline

From framing the shot to the final colour grade, I handle photography and film production myself, start to finish.

03

Always Learning

Every year has meant a new skill — manual mode, directing, colour grading, and now motion graphics.

Timeline

How this started

2022–23

First camera

Started with a Canon 1200D, mostly shooting school memories — the beginning of the journey.

2023

First film

Directed and edited a school project film, an adaptation of Romeo & Juliet — the moment filmmaking clicked as a strength.

2024

Learning manual mode

Learned manual camera settings and started documenting organization events; filmed two music videos in senior high school.

2025

Breakout year

Produced multiple music videos and a short thriller film, directed for the first time, and picked up Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, and Lightroom Classic.

2026

Sony A6700 & CSCSOC-HAU

Got the Sony A6700, joined CSCSOC-HAU as Media Head, and produced an advocacy short film for a national video competition.
